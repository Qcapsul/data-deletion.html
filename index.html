QBank Capsule – Account Deletion

QBank Capsule allows users to create an account to access educational question-bank content.

You can delete your account directly from the QBank Capsule app through the Profile section.

How to delete your account

If you can access the QBank Capsule app:

  1. Open QBank Capsule.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the account deletion request.

Can't access the app?

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

Please send your account email address and write "QBank Capsule Account Deletion Request" in the subject of your email.

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted?

When an account deletion request is completed, the QBank Capsule account and the personal information associated with that account are deleted from our systems, subject to any data that may need to be retained where legally required.

This may include:

  • Account information
  • Name
  • Mobile number, if provided
  • Session information, if provided
  • Registration information, if provided
  • College information, if provided
  • Associated profile information

Processing time

Account deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after the request is received and verified.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Once the account has been deleted, it may not be possible to recover the account or its associated information.
